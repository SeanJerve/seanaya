# Design Skill: Kawaii Pastel Romance & Digital Scrapbook UI

## 1. Design Philosophy
This website is a private digital space shared exclusively by two people (Sean & Aya). It is an intimate, lovely archive of their relationship, memories, experiences, milestones, and everyday interactions.

The interface must feel like:
- A personal digital diary.
- A beautifully maintained, tactile scrapbook.
- A cozy, pastel planner (Korean/Japanese stationery aesthetic).
- A little digital home with warmth, love, nostalgia, and charm.

## 2. Visual Language

### Color Palette
- **Baby Blue & Sky:** `#dbeafe`, `#e0f2fe`, `#e8f0fe` — Primary environmental background, airy and soft.
- **Powder Blue / Periwinkle:** `#bfdbfe`, `#c7d2fe` — Secondary surfaces, subtle pill highlights.
- **Soft Blush Pink:** `#fce7f3`, `#fbcfe8`, `#f472b6`, `#fb7185` — Romantic accents, hearts, countdowns, and active states.
- **Lavender & Lilac:** `#ede9fe`, `#ddd6fe` — Gentle accents for milestones and badges.
- **Warm Ivory / Alabaster:** `#ffffff`, `#fffdfa`, `#faf8f5` — Content cards with soft pillowy glass.
- **Muted Navy / Indigo:** `#1e293b`, `#2c3e6b`, `#334155` — High-legibility primary text, gentle on the eyes.
- **Soft Slate-Blue:** `#64748b`, `#7c8ba1` — Secondary metadata, coordinates, and timestamps.

### Surfaces & Soft UI
- **Pillowy Cards:** Generously rounded corners (`rounded-3xl` / 28px–32px), with soft diffused shadows:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -5px rgba(186, 215, 255, 0.4), 0 4px 12px rgba(220, 230, 250, 0.5);`
  and delicate white frosted borders: `border: 1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`.
- **Tactile Washi Tape:** Decorative pastel taped edges on Polaroids (`bg-pink-300/60`, `bg-sky-300/60`, angled tape strips).
- **Polaroid Keepsakes:** Classic instant-film photo cards with white borders, drop shadows, and subtle tilts.
- **Pillowy Inset Wells:** Slightly recessed surfaces (`bg-sky-50/60 shadow-inner`) for timers, counters, and progress meters.

### Typography & Annotations
- **Romantic Serif:** Elegant serif (Fraunces / Cormorant / Playfair) for names, titles, and emotional statements:
  `"Welcome back, Sean. ♡"`
  `"another day, another page in our story ♡"`
- **Friendly Sans:** Clean, warm sans-serif for controls, labels, and descriptions.
- **Handwritten / Script Touches:** Delicate cursive phrases (*"you & me always ♡"*), heart glyphs (`♡`), and tiny sparkles (`✧`).

## 3. Signature Details
- **White Lilies:** The couple's signature flower (`/main-lily.png`, `/lily1.png`, `/lily2.png`), placed as soft corner watermarks and bouquet accents.
- **Cute Cats:** Adorable illustrated kittens (`/couple-sticker.png`, SVG cat peeking over milestone progress, sleeping on clouds).
- **Looped Heart Connection:** Romantic looped pink ribbon line linking Sean in Bacolod to his partner across the miles.
- **Polaroids with Washi Tape:** Partner Chronicle and Photo Booth previews presented as tactile instant photos.
